## About me

I'm an Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka. I enjoy connecting hardware and software, from electronic circuits and FPGA designs to computer vision and machine learning experiments.

- 🧠 My computer vision work includes image classification, geometric fitting, image alignment, and depth reconstruction experiments.
- 🫀 My project work includes wearable ECG monitoring, combining embedded hardware with signal processing and machine learning.
- ⚙️ I also work on digital design, PCB design, analog electronics, and robotics.
- 🔬 I'm interested in research that brings intelligent algorithms into practical engineering systems.
