## 👨‍💻 A little about me

I'm an engineering undergraduate who enjoys bringing **hardware and software together**. My work spans electronic circuits, FPGA implementations, robotics, and computer vision experiments, with an interest in making intelligent systems work in the physical world.

- 🧠 **Vision & learning:** image classification, geometric fitting, image alignment, and depth reconstruction experiments.
- 🫀 **Engineering for health:** wearable ECG monitoring with embedded hardware, signal processing, and machine learning.
- ⚙️ **Hands-on design:** Verilog, testbenches, PCB design, analog electronics, and robotic systems.
- 🔬 **Research interests:** efficient computer vision, embedded intelligence, and practical digital system design.
- 🛠️ **Project contributions:** PCB and circuit design, component selection, hardware integration, and team coordination.
- 🤝 **Beyond projects:** SLRC 2026 competition organization and school workshops through the Electronic Club (2025/26).
